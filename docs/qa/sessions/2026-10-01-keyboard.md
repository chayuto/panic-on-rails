# Session: the keyboard-only player who prefers reduced motion (2026-10-01)

- **Charter:** build and run the M1 oval with the keyboard alone. Nothing may flash or shake.
- **Persona:** a player who uses the keyboard only, and has asked their system for less
  motion.
- **Setup:** fresh storage, collection mode, 1440 × 900, reduced motion emulated
  (`set-reduced-motion reduce`), the dev server, through `playwright-cli`.
- **Budget:** about 50 key presses.

## What happened

1. Tabbed through the page. The order runs through the toolbar, the parts bin's mode and scale
   tabs, the parts area (as a scrolling region), then the hint's buttons, then back to the top.
2. **No part card ever takes the focus**, and no key lays a piece. Building needs a drag, so
   this player can't build track at all (finding K1).
3. Opened the shop by its toolbar button with Enter, and tabbed to M1's "Build this layout":
   Enter built it, and the train ran. Space paused it. So a keyboard player can build a set's
   layout, but only that.
4. After the build, the focus was on the page body, not back on the shop button (finding K2).
   The next Tab starts again from the top of the page.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Heuristic | Finding | Outcome |
|---|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| K1 | 3 | WCAG 2.1.1 Keyboard | Track can only be laid by dragging: the part cards never take the focus. | A focused part card lays its piece: Enter at the end of the track (or in the middle of the view on a bare baseboard), Left or Right arrow turning a curve that way. |
| K2 | 2 | WCAG 2.4.3 Focus Order | After a dialog closes, the focus is on the page body. | The shop and the shopping list give the focus back to whatever opened them (`useDialogFocus`). |

**Tests:** `e2e/keyboard.spec.ts` lays three straights and a curve from a focused card, and
closes the shop to find the focus back on its button. `utils/__tests__/placePiece.test.ts`
covers laying from the keyboard. Both fail on the code before the fix.
