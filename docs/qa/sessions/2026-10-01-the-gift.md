# Session: the gift (2026-10-01)

- **Charter:** open the box, get the train running, earn, and buy the next box, going by
  what's on the screen alone.
- **Persona:** a new player who got a train set as a gift.
- **Setup:** fresh storage, collection mode (the M1 box and $20), 1440 × 900, the dev server.
  Played through `playwright-cli` 0.1.22: snapshot refs for the toolbar and dialogs, `look()`
  for the canvas, and mouse commands at its page coordinates.
- **Budget:** about 40 actions. Used about 35.

## What happened

1. The first screen:
   - The parts bin is "My collection": M1's sixteen pieces, and $20.
   - A hint says "Drag a track from the left to start building!"
   - The toolbar is icons only.
2. Dragged an R315 curve onto the canvas with the mouse. It placed, and the hint moved on to
   "Keep connecting tracks to form a loop".
3. Impatient, switched to Simulate with the one curve, and pressed Run on the diesel.
   - The train went on anyway, its last car off the end of the rails (finding 1).
   - Pressed Space: it bounced between the curve's two ends, four times in three seconds.
4. Add Train, with the one train already out: the shop opened on its Trains tab, to buy
   another. As designed.
5. Opened the shop by its box icon. M1 said "Owned ×1", with "Build this layout".
   - Built it, over the curve, through the "Replace layout" check.
   - The train ran, and the completion toast said to click a signal or a switch (finding 2).
6. Watched it earn: $20.00 to $25.01 in about 25 seconds.
   - That's about $12 a minute, above the design's $8–10. The play metrics hold the
     headless figure to that budget, so this is the live loop's timing, not a finding.
7. The train panel listed the train as "Train 2", while the same panel called it "Diesel
   passenger train" (finding 3).

## Findings

| # | Severity | Heuristic | Finding | Outcome |
|---|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| 1 | 3 | Error prevention | A train longer than its track was put on anyway. Its last car was drawn off the rails, and it bounced for ever. | Fixed in #182. A train goes only where all its cars stand on the track, and the panel says when there's no room. |
| 2 | 1 | Match with the real world | The completion toast said to click a signal or a switch; M1 has neither. | It says what the tools do, without assuming the layout has them. |
| 3 | 1 | Consistency | One train, two names in one panel: "Train 2" and "Diesel passenger train". | The panel names a train by its model, numbered when two of one model run. |
| 4 | 2 | Recognition rather than recall | Nothing points a new player to their box. The only hint is to drag track, and "Build this layout" sits behind an unlabelled box icon. | The first hint says to drag track, or to open the shop (its icon shown) and build the box's layout. |

**Evidence for finding 1:** `look()` gave `pieces: ["Curve R315-45°"]` and one train. It
stood on a 247 mm curve and is 412 mm long. The screenshot showed its third car level with
the canvas, off the end of the rails; running, the log was four `bounce` events.

**Tests.** Each finding has one; each fails on the code before its fix.
- Finding 1: Vitest (`standingSpot`, `rerail`, `addTrain`), and the e2e "a train too long for
  the track isn't put on it, and the panel says why".
- Finding 3: `collection.spec.ts` reads each train's name in the panel.
- Finding 4: `collection.spec.ts` looks for the first hint's pointer to the shop.
- Finding 2 is a change of words, and has no test.

## Notes for the tools

- A click read right after building a layout can miss. The view re-fits as the train panel
  replaces the parts bin. Read the coordinates until they hold still, as the e2e helpers'
  `clickTrain` does.
- Mouse drags from the parts bin work with raw `mousedown` and `mousemove` commands; HTML5
  drag and drop fires.
