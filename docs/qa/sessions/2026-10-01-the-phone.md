# Session: the phone-size player (2026-10-01)

- **Charter:** open the shop, build M1, run the train, stop it.
- **Persona:** a player on a phone-size screen.
- **Setup:** fresh storage, collection mode, 390 × 844, the dev server, through `playwright-cli`.
- **Budget:** about 20 actions.

## What happened

1. The first screen:
   - The toolbar wrapped to four rows.
   - The parts bin took 220 of the 390 px, leaving the canvas 170 px.
   - The first hint was squeezed against the right edge.
   - An orange "works best on desktop or tablet" box sat over the canvas.
2. The shop works well at this width: one card per row, "Build this layout" in reach.
3. Built M1. In Simulate the train panel took 300 px, leaving the canvas 90 px wide.
   - The fitted layout was a sliver at zoom 0.1.
   - `look()` put the running train off screen (`onScreen: false`), so the player couldn't see
     their train.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Heuristic | Finding | Outcome |
|---|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| 1 | 3 | Visibility of system status | In Simulate the train panel left the canvas 90 px wide: the layout a sliver, the train off screen. | Under 700 px the canvas takes the whole width, and the panel sits under it. |
| 2 | 2 | Flexibility and efficiency | In Edit the parts bin took 220 of 390 px. | The same: the parts bin sits under the canvas. |
| 3 | 2 | User control and freedom | The "works best on desktop or tablet" box covered the layout and couldn't be closed. | A slim bar at the foot of the canvas, with a close button. |
| 4 | 1 | Match with the real world | The first hint was squeezed to the right, and said "from the left" with the parts below. | On a phone it spans the canvas, and says "from the parts bin". |
| 5 | 1 | Aesthetic and minimalist design | The toolbar wraps to four rows, about 180 px. | Not fixed: noted. |

**Test:** `app-smoke.spec.ts` builds M1 at 390 × 844. It checks that the canvas takes the
whole width, that the train is in view, and that the warning closes. On the old CSS it fails,
with the canvas 90 px wide.
